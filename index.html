🌿 Why Are Trees Important?

🌬️

Clean Air

Trees absorb carbon dioxide and help keep our air fresh.

🐘

Protect Wildlife

Forests are homes for thousands of animal and bird species.

🌧️

Support Nature

Trees support the water cycle and protect healthy soil.

🌎

Fight Climate Change

Forests store carbon and help protect our planet.
