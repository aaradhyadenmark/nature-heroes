Mission 02

💧 Save water, protect tomorrow

Clean water is precious. Saving water also helps reduce the energy and resources used to treat and move it.

At home

  • Turn off the tap while brushing your teeth.
  • Take shorter showers where possible.
  • Run washing machines with full loads.
  • Tell an adult when you notice a leak.
  • Water gardens when it is cooler, following local guidance.

In your community

  • Never throw rubbish, oil or chemicals into drains or waterways.
  • Use refillable bottles where safe drinking water is available.
  • Join a local river, beach or park clean-up.
  • Learn about water challenges in your region.
  • Share water-saving ideas with friends and family.

For classrooms and communities

Schools can grow a greener future

Teachers, students, families and organisations can use these ideas for free. No account or sign-up is needed.

🏫

Classroom eco-team

Choose a weekly water and energy monitor, create reminder posters and celebrate practical improvements.

🪴

Plant and observe

Grow a suitable native plant, record its changes and learn what it needs to thrive.

🌏

Global nature lesson

Compare local forests and water sources in different countries, using reliable educational sources.

Safety first: get permission before planting or organising a clean-up. Follow local environmental guidance and never handle unknown waste, chemicals or unsafe water.
